## Design Specificity Verdict

### LLM Assessment
Apex Sports & Physical Therapy has a distinct brand positioning: "The Performance Sanctuary" (doctoral athletic science paired with serene, warm architectural restraint: `#F9F8F6` linen canvas, authoritative `#1B382B` Alpine Forest Pine, `#C25E3E` terracotta clay, Roman arches, and pure flat tonal layering). 

The booking funnel captures the clinical taxonomy (doctoral credentials, sports rehabilitation services, direct access, and upfront fee-for-service pricing), but its spatial and interaction execution collapses into an off-the-shelf SaaS wizard (Calendly/Jane App floating card) that feels category-interchangeable. It lacks architectural presence, clinical photography, and the Roman arch framing that gives the marketing homepage its doctoral dignity. Furthermore, it neglects the crucial trust bridge required for an out-of-network clinic charging $175–$195/session.

### Deterministic Scan & Browser Evidence
- **Static Scan**: 3 advisory violations for `design-system-font-size` (`text-[10px]` in `BookingStepDateTime.vue` lines 79, 114, 140) violating the documented minimum label ramp of 11px.
- **Headless Runtime DOM & Contrast Audit**:
  - **9 Low-Contrast WCAG AA Failures**: `text #c25e3e on #f4ece7` (3.6:1 vs 4.5:1 required) across status badges in `BookingStepService.vue` and `BookingSummaryRibbon.vue`; `text #768179 on #ffffff` (4.0:1) and on `#f9f8f6` (3.8:1) for secondary labels and compliance copy.
  - **2 All-Caps Readability Failures**: 38 and 53-character uppercase strings in `BookingStepService.vue` and `index.vue`.
  - **1 Skipped Heading Level**: DOM outline jumps from `<h2>` to `<h4>`.
  - **1 Overused Font Rule Flag**: Flagged Plus Jakarta Sans (90% usage); assessed as a contextual false positive because `DESIGN.md` explicitly mandates Plus Jakarta Sans for all operational UI.
- **Elevation System Drift**: 18 instances of decorative drop shadows (`shadow-md`, `shadow-sm`, `shadow-xs`) directly violating DESIGN.md's "Zero Decorative Shadow Rule".
- **Semantics**: `BookingStepper.vue` contains non-interactive `<li>` step triggers and invalid direct `<div>` children inside `<ol>`.

## Overall Impression
The booking funnel has a rock-solid data model, excellent query-param deep-linking, and transparent pricing. However, it undermines Apex's elite positioning through generic SaaS card styling, high cognitive density on the scheduling step, WCAG AA contrast failures, and an abrupt, anxiety-inducing checkout experience that fails to explain payment timing and superbills.

## What's Working
1. **Transparent Clinical Taxonomy & Upfront Pricing**: Clearly articulates doctoral initial assessment, post-op surgical protocols, 75-min biomechanical running analysis, and recovery suite with upfront pricing ($175, $195, $95) and dedicated 1-on-1 durations.
2. **Persistent Contextual Summary Ribbon**: `BookingSummaryRibbon.vue` provides consistent orientation across steps, anchoring service, clinician, appointment time, and HSA/FSA eligibility.
3. **Robust Foundation via Reka UI & Deep-Linking**: Clean state sync with route query parameters allows seamless routing directly from marketing links into specific services and specialists.

## Priority Issues (P0–P3)

### [P0] Form Accessibility & Autocomplete Breakdown in Step 3
- **Why it matters**: Inputs in `BookingStepPatient.vue` lack `id` attributes and `<label for="...">` associations. Clicking labels fails to focus inputs, and screen readers cannot announce field names. Missing browser `autocomplete` attributes (`given-name`, `family-name`, `email`, `tel`) forces manual typing, heavily penalizing mobile users and injured patients.
- **Fix**: Add unique `id`s and `for` bindings to all form fields. Add standard `autocomplete` attributes. Implement clear `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-pine/20` states per DESIGN.md.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] WCAG AA Contrast Failures on Clinical Badges and Secondary Copy
- **Why it matters**: 9 text elements fail WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum contrast. Terracotta text (`#C25E3E`) on soft clay (`#F4ECE7`) yields 3.6:1, and charcoal-light (`#768179`) on white yields 4.0:1. This causes eye strain and illegibility for visually impaired patients or outdoor mobile users.
- **Fix**: Use `text-clay-dark` (`#8E3F27`, 5.4:1 contrast) on light clay badges per DESIGN.md token rules. Upgrade informational charcoal labels to `text-charcoal-muted` (`#525B54`, 5.8:1 contrast).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] Step 2 Multi-Task Cognitive Overload & Forced Clinician Selection
- **Why it matters**: Step 2 forces three simultaneous high-stakes decisions: Doctor, Date, and Time Slot. Forcing patients to choose a specific doctor without an "Any Specialist (First Available)" option induces choice paralysis for patients who simply want the earliest available care.
- **Fix**: Introduce an "Any Specialist (First Available)" option. Reveal time slots conditionally only after date selection to respect Miller's Law (≤4 options per decision).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] High-Stakes Commitment Anxiety: Missing Payment Terms & Superbill Reassurance
- **Why it matters**: Step 3 asks for contact details and immediately presents "Confirm Appointment" without clarifying whether credit cards are charged now, how cancellation works, or how out-of-network superbills are delivered.
- **Fix**: Add a trust guarantee card directly above the CTA clarifying: (1) "No prepayment required today — payment collected post-treatment", (2) "Itemized Superbill with CPT codes provided within 24 hours", (3) "Free cancellation up to 24 hours prior".
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Pervasive Drop Shadows Violating the Flat Sanctuary Design System
- **Why it matters**: 18 instances of `shadow-md`, `shadow-sm`, and `shadow-xs` directly violate DESIGN.md's "Zero Decorative Shadow Rule". Paired with off-brand `emerald-*` and `amber-500` utilities and serif card headings in Step 1, it dilutes Apex's bespoke architectural identity.
- **Fix**: Strip all `shadow-*` utility classes. Structure hierarchy through 1px `border-ecru-border` and flat `#FFFFFF` on `#F9F8F6` tonal layering. Standardize card titles on Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

### Jordan (Confused First-Timer / Injured Patient Seeking Reassurance)
- **Clinician Paralysis**: Confronted with doctoral acronyms (OCS, SCS, FAAOMPT) without explanation or guidance on which doctor treats their condition.
- **Financial Panic**: Sees "$175" in the ribbon with no statement on whether payment is due now or at the clinic.
- **Phantom Referral Checkbox**: Checks "I have a physician referral / surgical operative report", but nothing happens; left wondering if paperwork must be brought physically.

### Sam (Accessibility-Dependent / Screen Reader & Keyboard User)
- **Unlabeled Form Fields**: Step 3 inputs have no programmatic label connection (`id`/`for`). NVDA/VoiceOver announces unlabelled edit fields.
- **Stepper Keyboard Traps**: Stepper items use `@click` on non-focusable `<li>` elements without `role="button"` or `tabindex="0"`.
- **Invalid List Structure**: Direct `<div>` connector lines inside `<ol>` invalidate DOM semantics.
- **Disappearing Step Names**: Stepper text labels are hidden on mobile (`hidden sm:inline`), leaving screen reader / zoom users with isolated numbers.

### Alex / Post-Op Athlete (High-Performance Biomechanical Patient)
- **No Autocomplete**: Missing browser autofill forces manual typing on mobile.
- **Omission on Post-Op Card**: Post-Op surgical card omits the "Superbill provided for OON insurance" bullet (only present on Service #1).
- **Dead-End Confirmation**: No "Add to Google/Apple Calendar" (.ics) action or surgeon protocol document upload on Step 4.
